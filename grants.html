American Heart Association (AHA)
Causal Neuro-Symbolic AI for Stroke Reperfusion Outcome Prediction

This project applies the Counterfactual Causal Knowledge Graph (C-CKG) framework to molecular pathway intervention in NHE9-mediated stroke reperfusion injury. By modeling causal relationships in neurological recovery pathways, the work aims to identify actionable intervention points that improve post-stroke outcomes — bridging causal AI with translational neuroscience.

MIDAS – Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society
Counterfactual Causal Knowledge Graphs (C-CKGs) for Root Cause Analysis and Actionable Intervention

Develops C-CKG — a unified graph-neural framework combining symbolic causal structure with deep learning — and validates it across three high-impact domains: predictive maintenance in smart manufacturing (AI4I 2020 dataset), NHE9 molecular pathway intervention in stroke reperfusion, and 2D copper nanoparticle synthesis optimization for sustainable catalysis. The project targets actionable causal insights that go beyond prediction to enable intervention.

📅 Period: 2026–2027 (proposed) 👤 Role: PI 🏛️ Program: MIDAS PODS 2026

University of Michigan Bold Challenges Initiative
A2D2: Ann Arbor–Detroit Digital Twins for Transportation and Supply Chain Resilience

Proposes a city-scale digital twin of the Ann Arbor–Detroit (A2D2) transportation corridor integrating supply chain resilience, cyber-physical security, and adaptive infrastructure policy. The project deploys Graph Neural Networks, Physics-Informed Neural Networks, and Federated Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning across the Mcity testbed and SEMCOG regional data infrastructure. Targets a Boost Award as a bridge toward an NSF Infrastructure Systems and People submission in Fall 2026.

📅 Period: 2026 (Boost phase) 👤 Role: Lead PI 🏛️ Partners: Mcity · Ross School of Business · UM-Ann Arbor
